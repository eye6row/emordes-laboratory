AboutEML / Est. 2026 / San Francisco

A textile research unit where Filipino weaving heritage meets the materials of the future.

EMORDES LABORATORY studies cloth at both ends of time. The optical wave of Binakol, the translucent hand of Piña, the tensile strength of Abaca sit in the same library as mycelium leather, algae knits and recycled organza.

Every specimen is catalogued, swatched and available for designers, students and brands developing their next collection. Request samples, book a lab session, or commission a custom weave study.
